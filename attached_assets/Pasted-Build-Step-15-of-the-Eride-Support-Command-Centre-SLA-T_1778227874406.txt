SLA Rules:

Support review SLA:
- urgent: first review within 30 minutes
- high: first review within 2 business hours
- medium: first review same business day
- low: first review within 2 business days

Engineering fix SLA:
- urgent: fix within 4 business hours once escalated to engineering
- high: fix within 1 business day once escalated to engineering
- medium: fix within current cycle / 7 days
- low: fix within next cycle / 14 days

For MVP, approximate business hours using normal elapsed time if business-hours calculation is too complex.
Prefer clean, simple logic over over-complication.

SLA calculation logic:

Create helper:
calculateTicketSla(ticket)

Inputs:
- priority
- internalStatus
- publicStatus
- createdAt
- updatedAt
- resolvedAt
- closedAt
- status history if needed

Rules:

1. Closed/completed tickets:
If publicStatus or internalStatus is:
- resolved
- closed
- duplicate
- not_a_bug
- spam

Then slaStatus = completed.

2. Paused tickets:
If internalStatus = needs_user_info or publicStatus = more_info_needed
Then slaStatus = paused.

3. First review SLA:
Applies when ticket is:
- new
- triage_required
- support_review

Due date calculated from createdAt based on priority.

4. Engineering SLA:
Applies when internalStatus is:
- engineering_escalation_required
- linear_created
- in_engineering
- in_review
- in_qa_verification
- fixed_waiting_user_notification

Due date calculated from the first time the ticket entered engineering_escalation_required or linear_created.
If status history is not enough, use updatedAt as fallback.

5. Approaching breach:
If current time is within 25% of remaining SLA window or within:
- urgent: 15 minutes before due
- high: 1 hour before due
- medium: 1 day before due
- low: 2 days before due

Then slaStatus = approaching_breach.

6. Breached:
If current time > due date and ticket is not completed or paused:
slaStatus = breached
slaBreachedAt = now if not already set.
